Privacy policy

truenas-rclone is used only by its owner to synchronise the owner's own files between a private NAS and the owner's own Google Drive account.

  • Google Drive data is accessed solely to copy, update and delete the owner's files in that sync.
  • No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to anyone else.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own NAS.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
